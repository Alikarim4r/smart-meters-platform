Delete your Smart Meters account

Sign in to either the Meter Entry app or the Dashboard app, open Settings, choose Delete account, and enter the displayed confirmation phrase.

Deletion permanently removes your sign-in, name, email, phone, company, profile photo, notifications, and assigned access. You will be signed out. Operational meter readings and required business audit history are retained without your personal profile attached.

If you cannot sign in, email Support@alielhassan.com from the address registered to your account and request deletion.
