Smart Meters Platform Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Smart Meters Platform applications: Meter Admin, Meter Entry, and Smart Meters Dashboard.

Information we process

Depending on your role and organization configuration, the apps may process account information such as name and email address, organization/site assignments, utility meter records and readings, notes, operational evidence or photos that you choose to attach, app settings, and subscription or purchase verification status. The apps may also process technical information required for authentication, security, synchronization, diagnostics, and notifications.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce role- and site-based permissions, record and review utility readings, administer organizations/sites/meters, generate operational reports, synchronize data across authorized devices, deliver operational notifications, and verify subscription entitlements where applicable.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the platform, such as hosting/authentication infrastructure and Google Play services for Android distribution and purchase verification, or when required by law. Organization data is restricted by server-side access controls to authorized users and scopes.

Security

The platform uses authenticated access, row-level authorization, scoped storage controls, encrypted network transport, server-side entitlement verification, and audit mechanisms. No security measure is absolute, and users should protect their account credentials and devices.

Retention and deletion

Account holders can request or initiate deletion from supported app settings. Personal profile data is deleted or anonymized as applicable. Some operational and audit records may be retained where necessary for security, integrity, contractual, or legal purposes. Account deletion instructions are available at Delete your Smart Meters account.

Children

The Smart Meters Platform is a business/utility operations product and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product, legal requirements, or service providers change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Support@alielhassan.com.
